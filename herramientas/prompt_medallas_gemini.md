# Prompt maestro (Gemini) — medallas 7 a 19
Usar la imagen de referencia de las 6 medallas actuales (Lapacho, Escarabajo, Siete Colores, Puño, Rayo, Llama de hielo).

Create a sprite sheet of 13 gym badges for a Pokémon-style game set in Jujuy, Argentina. Pixel-art, same style, outline, shading and level of detail as the reference badges. Each badge is a single icon, centered, about 96x96 px, thick dark outline, 3-4 tone shading, small white sparkle highlight, flat solid pure white background (#FFFFFF) so it can be cut out. Layout: 5 columns, 3 rows, equal spacing, no text, no labels, no numbers. Each badge has a different SHAPE (round, shield, diamond, leaf, hexagon, star, drop, crest) and its own dominant color. Badges 13-19 are the ELITE ones: gold frame, more ornate, slightly bigger details. Badges in order:

8  Humahuaca – dark purple ghost flame/mask with glowing yellow eyes, carnival confetti. Shape: shield.
9  Palpalá – grey steel gear with a blast-furnace flame in the middle. Shape: hexagon.
10 Pozuelos – pink flamingo over blue lagoon water ripples. Shape: drop.
11 Salinas Grandes – white salt crystals with a purple poison drop on cracked earth. Shape: round.
12 Maimará – the "Paleta del Pintor" striped colored hill on a cream badge, paint brush. Shape: round.
13 Volcán – orange volcano erupting with a small dragon wing silhouette. Shape: crest. (ELITE)
14 Quebrada – yellow lightning bolt wrapped around a red fist. Shape: star. (ELITE)
15 Dique La Ciénaga – teal storm cloud with rain and a small lightning bolt over water. Shape: round. (ELITE)
16 Cerro Zenta – violet mountain peak with a glowing third eye and a crescent. Shape: diamond. (ELITE)
17 Valle de las Sombras – black and silver moon with a ghost smile and two white eyes. Shape: shield. (ELITE)
18 Cielo y Mar – sky blue wings over ocean waves, rainbow-silver feather. Shape: crest. (ELITE)
19 El Sol – golden sun with a flame bird (phoenix) in the center, rainbow rays. Shape: star, the most ornate of all. (ELITE)
